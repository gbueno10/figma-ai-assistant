## Tips for Better Naming 💡

### Do ✅
- Mention age: "puppy" or "adult"
- Mention color: "golden", "white", "black", etc.
- Mention action: "sitting", "running", "playing"
- Be specific: "golden retriever puppy sitting"

### Don't ❌
- Be too vague: "a dog" (gets defaults)
- Include irrelevant details: "on a sunny Tuesday"
- Use multiple dogs: "two dogs playing" (AI may get confused)
- Mix languages: "un perro running" (stick to English)

### Examples

| Bad Prompt | Better Prompt | Best Prompt |
|------------|--------------|-------------|
| `dog` | `A dog sitting` | `A golden retriever puppy sitting` |
| `puppy photo` | `A puppy portrait` | `A white husky puppy portrait` |
| `animal` | `A brown dog` | `A brown labrador adult running` |
| `pet` | `A black puppy` | `A black puppy playing with toy` |
